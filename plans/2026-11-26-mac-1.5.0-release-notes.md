# Sidekey for Mac 1.5.0

## Teams Meeting mode

The Teams layout now has two modes. **Chat** is the layout Teams starts in. **Meeting** holds the keys for video calls and webinars. The last two keys of both modes switch between them, and Sidekey remembers your mode when you switch away from Teams and back.

| Key | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Mute, Video, Raise Hand | Shift-Command-M, Shift-Command-O, Shift-Command-K |
| Share Tray, Share Toolbar | Shift-Command-E, Shift-Command-Space |
| Leave (press and hold) | Shift-Command-H |
| Captions | Shift-Command-A |
| Admit Lobby | Shift-Command-Y |
| Join Meeting, Calendar | Control-Command-K, Command-2 |
| Zoom Share In, Zoom Share Out, Reset Zoom | Option-Command-Equals, Option-Command-Minus, Option-Command-0 |

- The shortcuts come from Microsoft's published "Keyboard shortcuts for Microsoft Teams" for Mac, and all of them were tried in a live Teams meeting. Microsoft's list also gives Shift-Command-A to Accept Audio Call; in a meeting the shortcut turns on captions.
- The Meeting keys do not light with Teams' mute or camera state.
- The Chat mode's Next Section and Prev Section keys make way for the two mode keys. The rest of the Chat mode is unchanged, including End Call with its press and hold.
- If you changed, moved or removed either of those two keys in Edit Layouts, your version stays and the mode key does not appear in that slot. To reach the Meeting mode, choose **Teams** in Edit Layouts, select the last key and choose **Reset Key...**.
- **Teams: Meeting** appears as its own row in Edit Layouts.

## Pairing

- The pairing window now says to enter the code on your iPad or iPhone.

## iPad and iPhone

1.5.0 needs no change to the iPad and iPhone app; it works with the version on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820215459).

## Updating

Sidekey does not update itself. Quit Sidekey, download the new `Sidekey.dmg` and drag Sidekey to Applications again, replacing the old copy.
